HOW TO PLAY

Know the rules

  • Roll a sixA token needs a six to leave its yard.
  • Move your tokenAfter rolling, click one of the highlighted legal tokens.
  • Capture opponentsLanding on an opponent on a non-safe square sends it home.
  • Safe squaresStarred squares protect tokens from captures.
  • Extra turnsA six or a capture grants another turn.
  • Win the matchGet all four of your tokens to the finish first.

This version is a local pass-and-play game. All players share the same screen.
